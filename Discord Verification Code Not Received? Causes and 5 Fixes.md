# Discord Verification Code Not Received? Causes and 5 Fixes

Have you resolved a Discord account ban, only to find that you are not receiving verification codes? Does Discord repeatedly show verification errors when you try to log in, or even prevent you from accessing a server? The problem may be related to your network connection, contact information, SMS or email filtering, or request limits.

This guide explains the common reasons why Discord verification codes are not received and provides a five-step troubleshooting process to help you identify and fix the issue.

## **I. Why Does Discord Require Verification Codes?**

Discord does not require users to verify their identity every time they perform an action. Verification may be required when there are changes to an account's registration or login status, or when users perform certain actions. Common scenarios include:

-   **Account registration or information changes:** When creating a new account, adding an email address, or linking a phone number, Discord may ask users to verify their contact information to confirm that it is valid.
-   **Changes in login activity:** When accessing an account from a new device, browser, or unfamiliar login environment, Discord may require additional identity verification.
-   **Unusual account activity:** Frequent actions within a short period, repeated behavior, or other unusual activity may trigger additional security checks.
-   **Server verification requirements:** Some servers enable member verification levels. Users must meet the relevant requirements to gain full interaction permissions.
-   **Multi-factor authentication (MFA):** If MFA is enabled, users must complete the second verification step according to their account security settings when logging in.

It is important to distinguish between these verification methods. Email and phone verification are mainly used to confirm contact information, while MFA adds an extra layer of login security. Server verification levels are configured independently by individual servers. Identifying the type of verification you are dealing with is the first step in troubleshooting.

## **II. 4 Common Reasons Why Discord Verification Codes Are Not Received**

### **1. Network Connection or Verification Service Issues**

Discord verification requires a request to be sent to its servers and processed by the relevant service. If your network connection is unstable, DNS resolution fails, or your proxy settings are incorrect, the request may not be submitted successfully. This can cause the page to keep loading, show a sending error, or fail to provide a response.

Temporary outages affecting Discord services may also prevent verification requests from being processed, even when your local network is working normally.

### **2. Invalid Phone Number or Email Address**

Discord sends verification codes to the contact information provided by users. If the phone number has an incorrect country code, an invalid number of digits, or the email address contains a typo, the verification code may not reach the intended recipient.

Discord also does not support VoIP numbers, temporary burner numbers, or landline numbers. In addition, a phone number that is already linked to another Discord account may not be available for reuse.

### **3. SMS Messages or Emails Are Being Blocked**

If Discord indicates that a verification code has been sent but you still cannot find it, the issue may occur during message delivery.

On mobile devices, check whether your carrier blocks international SMS messages or whether spam filters and security apps are blocking incoming messages. For email, check your spam and promotional folders, as well as any automatic filtering rules or blocked sender settings.

### **4. Too Many Verification Requests**

Requesting verification codes repeatedly within a short period may trigger Discord's rate limits. The system may temporarily restrict further requests to prevent repeated verification attempts or unusual activity. As a result, you may be unable to receive another code even when your contact information is correct.

You may also encounter Rate Limited or Recently Used messages if your phone number has recently been used to verify another account. This usually indicates a request frequency or phone number usage restriction, rather than necessarily meaning that the number itself is invalid.

## **III. How to Fix Discord Verification Code Issues: 5 Steps**

### **Step 1: Maintain a Stable Network Connection**

First, check whether Discord's web version or desktop client can open normally and whether the verification page loads and submits requests successfully. An unstable connection may prevent verification requests from being submitted, cause the page to load continuously, or interrupt the process.

Follow these steps to troubleshoot your connection:

-   **Switch networks:** Try connecting through a different Wi-Fi network or mobile data to determine whether the current network is causing the issue.
-   **Check network settings:** Make sure DNS resolution is working properly and rule out incorrect proxy settings or connection interruptions.
-   **Check service status:** Visit Discord Statusto see whether services such as the API or Web are experiencing issues.

Beyond troubleshooting temporary network problems, it is also important to maintain a stable connection during regular Discord use. Frequently switching network environments may introduce changes to your account's access environment and affect normal access and verification.

### **Step 2: Check Your Phone Number and Email Address**

If the verification process cannot be completed, first check whether the contact information entered on Discord is correct. Incorrect or unsupported contact details may prevent verification codes from being delivered.

-   **Phone number:** Check the country code and number of digits, and make sure you have not entered the country code twice. Use a valid mobile carrier number whenever possible. Avoid VoIP numbers, temporary burner numbers, and landlines.
-   **Email address:** Check the spelling and domain of your email address, and make sure it matches the address linked to your Discord account.

If you see an Existing Account message, your phone number may already be linked to another Discord account. Check the existing account and, if necessary, manage the phone number through that account before trying again.

### **Step 3: Restore Normal SMS and Email Delivery**

If Discord indicates that a verification code has been sent but you have not received it, the problem may be related to message delivery.

-   **Mobile SMS:** Check your spam messages and blocked message records. Make sure your SIM card has a signal, and contact your carrier to confirm whether international or service-related SMS messages are restricted. If you use a dual-SIM phone, make sure the relevant SIM card is enabled.
-   **Email:** Check your inbox, spam folder, and promotional folder. Look for automatic filtering rules or blocked sender settings. You can also add Discord's official sender addresses to your email allowlist in advance.

After completing these checks, return to your Discord account settings and click Resend Verification Email to send the verification email again. If you still do not receive it, try using another email address.

### **Step 4: Handle Verification Rate Limits and Expired Codes**

If Discord displays Rate Limited or Recently Used, your verification request may be subject to a rate limit or phone number usage restriction. Repeatedly clicking the resend button is unlikely to solve the problem and may extend the waiting period.

Try the following:

1.  **Rate limit triggered:** Stop submitting repeated requests. For certain phone number verification restrictions, Discord recommends waiting 24–48 hours before trying again.
1.  **Invalid verification code:** Use the most recently received code instead of an older one.
1.  **Expired verification code:** Return to the verification page and request a new code. Complete verification promptly after receiving it.
1.  **Multiple verification pages open:** Close outdated verification pages and keep only one active session to avoid confusion between different requests.

### **Step 5: Use an Alternative Verification Method or Contact Discord Support**

If your current verification method is still unavailable, check whether your account has another enabled security option.

For example, accounts with MFA enabled may be able to use a time-based code generated by an authenticator app or an unused backup code. If SMS MFA was enabled previously, you may also be able to select SMS verification on the login verification page.

If you cannot access your account and have no available backup verification method, submit a ticket through the Discord Support Center. Include the error message, verification method, and time when the issue occurred to help the support team investigate.

## **IV. How to Prevent Discord Verification Issues**

### **1. Use Long-Term, Reliable Contact Information**

When registering or updating your account, use a phone number that you own and intend to keep, along with an email address you regularly access. After linking them, test whether both SMS messages and emails can be received normally.

Avoid temporary burner numbers, VoIP numbers, or email addresses that may become inaccessible. Otherwise, you may be unable to receive verification codes when logging in or recovering your account.

### **2. Set Up Your Account Security in Advance**

**Go to User Settings → My Account** in Discord and check whether your email address and phone number have been verified. Then enable MFA if needed and save the backup codes provided by Discord.

Store your backup codes separately in a password manager or another secure location. Do not keep them only on your currently logged-in device, as you may lose access to them if your phone is lost or replaced.

### **3. Manage Account Activity and Request Frequency**

When using Discord, avoid joining large numbers of servers, sending bulk direct messages, or repeatedly posting identical content within a short period.

If you manage multiple servers or accounts, schedule activities at reasonable intervals to reduce repetitive or concentrated actions. Also, use the official Discord client whenever possible and avoid modified third-party clients from untrusted sources.

## **V. FAQ**

**Q1: What should I do if I never receive my Discord verification email?**

A: First, confirm that the email address linked to your account is correct. Then check your spam and promotional folders, along with any email filtering rules. Add Discord's official sender addresses to your allowlist and resend the verification email through your account settings.

**Q2: How long does it take to receive a Discord SMS verification code?**

A: SMS delivery may be delayed due to carrier networks or international SMS routing. Wait a while and check your blocked messages before requesting another code. Avoid submitting repeated requests. If you see Rate Limited or Recently Used, follow Discord's recommendation to wait 24–48 hours.

**Q3: Can I use a virtual phone number to receive Discord verification codes?**

A: Not necessarily. Discord does not support VoIP numbers, temporary burner numbers, or landlines. Even if a virtual number can receive SMS messages from other platforms, that does not mean it will pass Discord verification.

## **VI. Conclusion**

If you are not receiving Discord verification codes, check your network connection, contact information, message delivery, and request limits one by one. To prevent future issues, maintain a stable network environment, keep your account security settings up to date, and avoid repeatedly requesting verification codes. If the problem persists after multiple attempts, contact Discord Support for assistance.
